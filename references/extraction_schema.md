# Extraction schema

Minimum fields:

- `record_id`, `review_status`, `source_type`, `full_text_status`
- authors, title, year, journal/issuer, DOI/URL, database/source
- country, region, World Bank income group, policy level
- health domain and technology domain
- policy/strategy name, policy objective, target population/sector
- CTI component, policy-cycle stage, policy instrument
- implementation actors, governance arrangement, funding/regulation/procurement
- empirical design or documentary basis, data source, sample/unit of analysis
- reported outputs, outcomes, impacts, barriers, facilitators
- IP present (`yes/no/unclear`), IP mechanism, patent/licensing/transfer/access
  details, and evidence locator
- reviewer 1 decision, reviewer 2 decision, adjudication, exclusion reason,
  extraction notes, and reviewer confidence

Use `not_reported` rather than filling missing information with assumptions.
Keep direct source statements separate from analyst interpretation.
