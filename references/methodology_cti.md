# Methodology configuration

## Initial thesis configuration

- Domain: health and medicine, including health technologies and the health
  technology industry.
- Main object: government policies, strategies, programmes, and interventions.
- Evidence requirement: empirical description, implementation evidence,
  evaluation, observed outputs/outcomes, case evidence, or documented policy
  experience. Pure theory and conceptual discussion are not eligible.
- Secondary IP lens: patents, intellectual property rules, licensing, technology
  transfer, access, and market-exclusivity mechanisms may be extracted when
  present; they are not required for inclusion.
- Comparison: country, region, income group, policy level, and policy-cycle
  stage may be compared when reported.

## Eligibility logic

Include a record only when it has:

1. a health or medical context;
2. a CTI policy, strategy, programme, or government intervention as an object;
3. empirical or documentary evidence beyond a purely conceptual proposal.

Exclude when the primary object is social innovation without a technological or
CTI-policy component, health technology assessment without policy/CTI analysis,
or a conceptual/theoretical framework without empirical application.

## Reviewer protocol

Reviewer 1 is the AI and reviewer 2 is the named human researcher. Store each
decision separately: `include`, `exclude`, `unclear`, or `not_assessed`.
Disagreements are not silently averaged. They remain open until the human
researcher adjudicates or records consensus.

The recommended pilot is 30 title/abstract records, followed by a documented
criterion revision if agreement is below the user's threshold. The thesis
configuration uses 80% agreement, but the user may change this.

## Synthesis lenses

Use only lenses supported by the included corpus:

- policy-cycle stage: agenda, formulation/decision, implementation, evaluation;
- policy instrument: funding, regulation, procurement, infrastructure,
  capability building, industrial support, coordination, access, IP/transfer;
- actors and governance level;
- country/region and World Bank income group when relevant;
- reported outputs, outcomes, impacts, barriers, and facilitators;
- innovation-system orientation: neoclassical, evolutionary, systemic, or
  transformative, only when stated or transparently inferred from the source.

Do not treat the presence of a policy as evidence of effectiveness.
