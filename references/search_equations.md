# Search-equation guidance

Build a separate query for each database and preserve the exact executed form.
Use three conceptual blocks when appropriate:

`(CTI policy terms) AND (health/medicine terms)`

The initial CTI block should cover innovation policy, science policy, technology
policy, industrial policy, research and development policy, science and
technology policy, and technology-based innovation policy. Add the following
as a secondary IP/transfer block only when the review needs higher sensitivity:

`"intellectual propert* polic*" OR "industrial propert* polic*" OR "patent polic*" OR "patent strateg*" OR "technology transfer polic*" OR "knowledge transfer polic*" OR "licensing polic*" OR "research commerciali*ation polic*" OR "technology commerciali*ation polic*" OR "open innovation polic*" OR "TRIPS"`

Health block:

`health* OR medic* OR biomedic* OR pharma* OR vaccin* OR biotech* OR "medical technolog*" OR "health technolog*" OR diagnost* OR device*`

Adapt field syntax, phrase rules, truncation, and proximity operators to the
database. Validate the query syntax in the target interface before reporting a
result count. A proposed equation is not an executed search.
